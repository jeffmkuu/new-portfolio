My Passion

I believe good work starts with listening. Before I write a single line of code or design a poster, I like to understand how the customer’s business really runs: what they sell, who works with them, and what slows them down every day. I build for small businesses, so I keep things simple, explain in plain language, and never make anyone feel lost because of technical words.

For me the relationship does not end when the work is delivered. I am honest about what can be done and how long it will take, I make time when someone needs help, and I keep improving a system until it truly works for them. My time in IT support at Kenyatta University taught me patience, and how much it means to a person when their problem is fixed with respect. I want every customer to feel they can call me, trust me and come back.
